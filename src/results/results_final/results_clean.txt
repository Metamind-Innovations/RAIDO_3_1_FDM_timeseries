KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The federated teacher improves over all local baselines: RMSE
   drops 10.4% and MAE drops 17.8% on the weighted average holdout.

2. The synthetic dataset preserves the structural (R²) quality of
   the real data almost exactly (gap only +0.003), meaning temporal
   periodicities are well captured.

3. Per-client asymmetry:
   - Client 2 (low mean 50.7, low variance): synthetic judge is
     BETTER than real (RMSE −15.2%, R² +0.011). The global synthetic
     mean (55.9) is close to C2, and the larger combined dataset
     provides additional regularization.
   - Client 1 (high mean 61.1, high variance): synthetic judge is
     worse (RMSE +26.1%). The global synthetic mean is 5.2 %RH
     below C1's local mean, and the lower synthetic variance (6.0
     vs 7.9) reduces coverage of C1's dynamic range.

4. The overall RMSE gap (+0.077) is moderate, showing meaningful
   knowledge transfer from the federated teacher into synthetic data,
   with room for improvement via client-adaptive distribution alignment.
